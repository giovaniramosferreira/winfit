# Prompts usados na ferramenta integrada de imagens

Modo: geração com referências de estilo/personagem, fundo branco. A primeira referência é a imagem enviada pelo usuário; flexão e leg press também recebem a folha original de agachamento como referência de identidade. Não há API/CLI alternativa.

## Agachamento

Use case: scientific-educational. Create an ORIGINAL animation sprite sheet for a fitness app, a bodyweight SQUAT. The supplied image is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: its expressive charcoal-gray pen contours, anatomically credible athletic adult body, blank simplified face, white skin/paper and irregular parallel hatching on pants. Do NOT reproduce its exact figure/pistol pose, screenshot, watermark or lettering. Original hand-drawn illustration, bold varied ink contour, natural muscle volume, organic connected limbs, articulate hands and sneakers, no geometric mannequin or visible mechanical joints. Same shirtless athletic adult man wearing knee-length hatched shorts and sneakers in every frame, three-quarter SIDE profile facing LEFT. White background. EXACT contact sheet 3 columns x 2 rows, six equal square cells, 1536x1024 composition. No grid lines, borders, labels, arrows, numbers, typography or shading outside the figure. Each cell contains ONE full-body person, with generous 8% padding, same identity, proportions, camera, overall scale and foot positions. Ground contact at the SAME height within each cell. Feet flat and planted, shoulder-width stance seen from side, both arms reaching forwards to LEFT. In reading order: frame1 upright with knees extended naturally; frame2 slight squat; frame3 halfway down; frame4 thighs approaching parallel; frame5 controlled deep comfortable squat near thigh-parallel; frame6 lowest controlled squat thighs parallel. Hips travel BACK to RIGHT while knees bend, spine stays neutral, chest gently leans forward, head follows torso naturally. Heels never leave ground. Arms, hands, knees and torso remain anatomically connected and realistic. Frame6 must NOT be a pistol squat: BOTH feet remain on ground. This is a filmstrip of one continuous descending squat, not six different exercises. CONSISTENT anchored feet and scale across all six cells are essential because these frames will be sliced and animated. Style should look like a professional hand-inked sports instruction book, with lineweight and hatching like the reference, no color, no gray filled mannequin shapes, no captions.

## Flexão

Use case: scientific-educational. ORIGINAL six-frame animation sprite sheet for a fitness app. Image1 is STYLE reference ONLY: expressive charcoal-gray hand-ink contours and irregular hatching on shorts, white paper, not its exact figure, watermark, lettering or pistol pose. Image2 is CHARACTER IDENTITY reference: use the same natural muscular adult man, blank simplified face, shirtless with knee-length hatched shorts and sneakers. Organic connected anatomy, natural proportions, detailed hands, bold varied pen contour, NOT a geometric mannequin, no mechanical joints. EXACT 3 columns x 2 rows of six equally sized square cells, landscape 1536x1024 sheet. Reading order left to right top row, then left to right bottom row. Each cell contains ONE complete pose, same character/camera/scale, generous margins, same support points relative to cell. Pure white background, NO text, labels, arrows, numbers, grid lines, panels, watermark or branding. This will be sliced into six animation frames; maintain identical support positions and scale, no unrelated poses. Exercise is STANDARD FLOOR PUSH-UP, strict SIDE profile facing LEFT, head at LEFT and toes at RIGHT, same side as character reference. View horizontal full body. Six successive phases of DESCENT, beginning with both arms straight, ending with elbows about 90 degrees and chest close to floor. Frame1 top full plank; frame2 slight elbow bend; frame3 35degrees; frame4 55degrees; frame5 75degrees; frame6 approximately90degrees at bottom. Hands flat FIXED under shoulders in every cell, toes FIXED touching the floor at exactly the same coordinates within each cell. Head, shoulder, hip and ankles form a neutral straight line, body lowers as ONE connected unit, NO sagging hips, NO kneeling, NO arched back. Elbows bend backward toward torso. The whole chest/hip line lowers naturally toward the planted hands, arm length remains constant. Draw complete hands, two overlapping arms, both feet, hatched shorts, human muscle definition and thick expressive ink. The head may shift very slightly as arms rotate but stays naturally aligned with spine.
